# prinsloo-ai
A collection of custom AI skills and MCPs

## Skills

Ready-to-use skills for AI assistants. Each one is a single `SKILL.md` file that works in Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, and teaches the assistant to do one task the same careful way every time. They are free to use and adapt under CC BY 4.0.

| Collection | Skills | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Business suite | 44 | Email, meetings, reports, research, customers, goals and people, planning, files |
| Creator suite | 13 | Writing, SEO, video scripts and captions, prompts for AI image and video tools, brand |
| Standalone | 2 | Small-business acquisition analysis, and rewriting text that sounds machine-written |
