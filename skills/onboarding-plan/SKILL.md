# Onboarding plan

**30-60-90** means learn, contribute, own. Each phase ends on goals a manager could observe, and week one ends with something real shipped.

## Gather
- The role, start date, manager and team. The job description if there is one.
- What the person needs to be doing independently by day 90.
- Systems, access, equipment and mandatory training.
- Who they need to know, inside and outside the team.

For a user planning their own first 90 days, write it from their side: what to learn, who to meet, what to deliver, what to ask the manager.

## Steps
1. `Before day one`: accounts, equipment, a welcome note, a buddy assigned, the first week's calendar sent.
2. `Week one`, day by day: introductions, setup, context, and a **first win**: a small real task finished by Friday.
3. `Days 1 to 30, learn`: three to five goals about understanding the business, the customers, the tools and the team.
4. `Days 31 to 60, contribute`: three to five goals built around a first deliverable the person owns.
5. `Days 61 to 90, own`: three to five goals describing independent results.
6. `People to meet`: each with the reason and a question to ask them.
7. `Check-ins`: weekly 1:1s and reviews at 30, 60 and 90 days, each with what will be discussed.
8. `Resources`: documents, training, channels.
9. Mark every logistics item nobody has confirmed as `[confirm: owner]`.

## Shape
```
<Name>, <role>, starts <date>. Manager. Buddy.
Before day one (checklist)
Week one | day | morning | afternoon |
30: learn | 60: contribute | 90: own  (goals and how each is observed)
People to meet | who | why | ask them |
Check-ins
```

## Done when
- Each phase has three to five goals, each observable by the manager.
- The first win and the first owned deliverable are named, with dates.
- Every person to meet has a reason.
- Day-one logistics are confirmed or flagged.
