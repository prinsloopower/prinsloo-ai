# Weekly plan

Choose three **big rocks** for the week and put them on the calendar first. Everything else fits around them or leaves the week.

## Gather
- The week's calendar, from the connected calendar or pasted.
- Open tasks and deadlines, from a connected task tool, a list or the user.
- Commitments sitting in the inbox, if a mail tool is connected.
- Goals for the quarter, and last week's plan if there was one.
- Working hours and the hours when the user focuses best. Ask if unknown.

## Steps
1. Review last week if a plan exists: done, carried, dropped.
2. Collect every open commitment into one list with its deadline.
3. Choose three big rocks: outcomes for this week that move a quarterly goal or meet a hard deadline. State each as a finished thing.
4. Compute capacity: working hours minus meetings, then keep 30% of what remains unplanned.
5. Block time for the big rocks first, in the user's best focus hours. Batch small tasks into one or two blocks.
6. Audit the meetings: flag any to decline, shorten or hand to someone else, and draft the note for each.
7. Sort what does not fit: defer with a new date, delegate to a named person, or drop. Say which, item by item.
8. Name one must-do for each day.
9. Present the plan. Change the calendar only after the user approves the changes.

## Shape
```
Big rocks: 1, 2, 3
Capacity: <hours> working, <hours> in meetings, <hours> plannable
| Day | Must-do | Focus blocks | Meetings |
Decline or shorten | meeting | suggestion | draft note |
Deferred | Delegated | Dropped
```

## Done when
- Each big rock has blocks on the calendar.
- Planned hours fit within capacity, with the arithmetic shown.
- Every open commitment is scheduled, deferred to a date, delegated to a person or dropped.
- Calendar changes and decline notes are proposals awaiting approval.
