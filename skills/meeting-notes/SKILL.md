# Meeting notes

Notes record **decisions** and **action items**. Discussion is kept only where a reader needs it to understand a decision.

## Gather
- The transcript or rough notes, pasted, attached or from a connected meeting tool.
- The agenda and attendee list, if they exist, to check that every item was covered.
- Earlier notes for the same meeting series, to close out previous actions.

## Steps
1. List **decisions**: what was decided, who decided, and what it replaces if it reverses something earlier.
2. List **action items**: a verb first, one named owner, a due date. Where the meeting left the owner or date unsaid, write `[owner?]` or `[due?]`.
3. List **open questions**: raised and not resolved, with who will resolve them.
4. Add a **parking lot** for topics deferred to another time.
5. Check previous actions if earlier notes exist: done, carried or dropped.
6. Write a summary of at most five lines at the top.
7. Write the follow-up message: summary, decisions, actions grouped by owner. Under 150 words.

## Shape
```
<Meeting>, <date>. Present: <names>
Summary (five lines)
Decisions      | decision | decided by |
Actions        | action | owner | due |
Open questions | question | owner |
Parking lot
---
Follow-up message
```

## Done when
- Every action has one named person and a date, or a visible `[owner?]` / `[due?]`.
- Every decision and action can be traced to a line in the transcript or notes.
- Every agenda item is accounted for, or noted as not covered.
- The follow-up is a draft for the user to send.
