# Performance review

Every statement is **SBI**: the situation, the behavior observed, the impact it had. A review describes what a person did. It makes no claims about what kind of person they are.

## Gather
- The branch: manager review, self-review or peer feedback.
- Goals and expectations for the period, and the level or role rubric if there is one.
- Evidence across the whole period: accomplishments, notes, 1:1 records, feedback from others, results.
- The organization's form, rating scale and word limits.

Examples come from the user's material. Where a strength or growth area has no example, ask for one or mark it `[example needed]`.

## Steps
1. Lay the evidence along a timeline of the period. Point out months with little evidence so recent events do not stand in for the whole year.
2. `Results against goals`: each goal with what was achieved and the measure.
3. `Strengths`: two or three, each with a dated example and its impact.
4. `Growth areas`: one to three, each with an example, the impact, and what better looks like.
5. `Next period`: goals and the support the manager will give.
6. `Summary`: three sentences consistent with everything above. Add a rating rationale only when the user gives the scale. The rating is the manager's decision.
7. Run the wording check and list each flagged phrase with a rewrite.
   - Personality labels ("abrasive", "not a natural leader") become the behavior and its impact.
   - Vague praise ("great team player") becomes an example.
   - The standard applied matches the person's level and matches what peers are held to.
   - Health, leave, family, age and other personal circumstances are removed.
8. Branch differences. Self-review: first person, results with numbers, misses stated with what was learned. Peer feedback: two strengths and one suggestion, each as SBI.

## Done when
- Every strength and growth area has a specific, dated example.
- Every sentence describes behavior or results.
- Evidence covers the whole period, or the gap is stated.
- The flagged-phrase list was produced, even if empty.
