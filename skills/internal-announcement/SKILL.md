# Internal announcement

Write an **inverted pyramid**: a reader who stops after the subject and first paragraph still knows whether they are affected and what to do.

## Gather
- What is changing, the effective date, and the reason leadership would give out loud.
- Who is affected, and whether groups are affected differently.
- The action required, its deadline, and where questions go.
- `business-context.md`, if one is in the working folder, project or attachments: it sets voice, brand, products, customers and competitors.

Ask once, in one message, for a missing effective date, action or contact.

## Steps
1. Subject: the change and the date. "New expense tool from 1 June: submit May claims by 28 May."
2. First paragraph: what changes, when, and the one thing readers must do.
3. `Who is affected`: one line per group when the impact differs. Say plainly who is unaffected.
4. `What you need to do`: numbered actions, each with a deadline.
5. `Why`: two or three sentences, including the cost of the change to employees if there is one.
6. `Questions`: the three questions people will really ask, including the uncomfortable one, each answered or marked `[answer needed]`.
7. Write a chat version: three lines and a pointer to the full note.

## Shape
```
Subject: <change> from <date>: <action> by <deadline>

<What changes, when, the one action.>

Who is affected
What you need to do
Why
Questions
Where to get help: <named person or channel>
```

## Done when
- Subject plus first paragraph answer "am I affected, and what do I do?"
- Every action has a deadline and every group has a line.
- The uncomfortable question is in `Questions`.
- Both versions are delivered as drafts. The user sends them.
