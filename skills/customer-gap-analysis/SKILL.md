# Customer gap analysis

Lay four things side by side: what the customer **needs**, what they **bought**, what they **use**, and what we **offer**. Every mismatch between two of them is a gap, and each kind of gap calls for a different owner.

| Gap | The mismatch | Usual owner |
|---|---|---|
| Adoption | Bought, barely used | Customer success |
| Whitespace | Needed and offered, not bought | Sales |
| Product | Needed, not offered | Product |
| Service | Offered and used, delivered poorly | Support or delivery |
| Relationship | No sponsor, or a single contact | Account lead |

## Gather
- Needs: goals the customer has stated, from call notes, emails, proposals and reviews.
- Bought: contracts, entitlements, renewal dates.
- Uses: usage data, logins, features adopted, support tickets.
- Offer: products and services, from `business-context.md` if one is in the working folder, project or attachments, or from the user.

Sources come from connected tools or files the user supplies. Name any of the four that is missing, and analyze what remains.

For a segment or a whole portfolio, run the same analysis per account and add a roll-up of gaps that repeat.

## Steps
1. List the customer's needs, each with its evidence: a quote, a ticket, a note with a date.
2. For each need, record whether we offer it, whether they bought it, and how much they use it.
3. Classify each need as **met** or as one gap type from the table.
4. Check the relationship separately: sponsor, number of contacts, last executive conversation.
5. Score each gap for impact (revenue at risk or expansion value) and effort to close, each high, medium or low.
6. Rank by impact over effort. For each gap write the next action, an owner and a date.
7. Summarize the product gaps on their own, worded for a product team.

## Done when
- Every stated need is marked met or carries exactly one gap type.
- Every gap has evidence, impact, effort and a next action.
- Revenue figures come from the user's records or show as placeholders.
- The analysis opens with the top three gaps and the renewal date.
